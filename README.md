A short, judge-directed entry: a one-star review of a Monday — the alarm
snoozed itself, the coffee filed for divorce, the inbox had triplets
overnight — and the judge, who survived their own Monday too, gets the
coffee on the house.

## The entry

**Monday: one star. My alarm snoozed itself, my coffee filed for divorce, and my inbox had triplets overnight. Judge, you survived yours too — the coffee is on me.**

## How it works

`main.py` writes the entry text to `/output/entry.txt` (or
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set). No network, no randomness, no
arguments: the entry is fixed, so every run produces the same bytes.
